Built for studying

Focus timer

Work in focused blocks, with a clock always in view.

Homework and reading

A homework list to tick off and a reading log that tracks pages and minutes.

Notes and files

A notepad and a file viewer for PDFs and Google Docs, Slides and Sheets links.

XP, levels and badges

Earn XP for steady practice, with daily limits and streaks, and collect badges as you level up.

Journey

A private mood and journal space. The journal stays on the device; only the day's mood word is shared.

Safe for children by design

No email, no account

Students join with a class code, their name and a 4-digit PIN. There is no student email or Google account.

Nothing for sale

StudentDeck never shows a price or a purchase link. Locked tools simply say to ask a grown-up at home.

Private by default

Journal text never leaves the device, and photos are cleaned of location data when added.

StudentDeck questions

Does StudentDeck work without a class?

Yes. It is a complete study dashboard on its own. Joining a class adds points, rewards and the teacher's calendar.

How does a student join a class?

With the class code from the teacher, their name and a 4-digit PIN. No email address is needed.

How do students get Pro?

Through a family's ParentDeck Pro plan or the school's Suite plan. Students never pay or see prices.

Which languages does it support?

The interface is in English, and the Translate tool covers 14 languages including Vietnamese.
