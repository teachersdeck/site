What your university gets

Suite plans give each faculty all four apps.

TeachersDeck Pro for every lecturer

Every lecturer seat on a faculty plan includes TeachersDeck Pro: attendance, assignments, polls, a whiteboard and more.

StudentDeck for every student

Students join a course with a class code and a PIN, and keep a focus timer, homework list and notes in one place.

ParentDeck for families far away

Students often live far from home. Parents follow homework, attendance and lecturer notes in real time, read-only.

AdminDeck for faculty leaders

Deans and administrators see every course: activity, homework completion, join codes, rosters and people.

Questions from universities

Why do universities pay seat fees?

Like international schools, universities pay a faculty plan plus a yearly seat fee: 100,000 VND per student and 500,000 VND per parent who links with ParentDeck.

Do we pay for every parent?

No. Parent seats count only the families who link with ParentDeck to follow their student.

How do discounts work across faculties?

Each faculty plan counts as one campus: 10% off from 4 plans, 15% off with premium support from 10, and 20% off or better from 25. A two-year agreement takes another 5% off. Discounts apply to the faculty plans.

Our faculty is bigger than Suite Growth. What then?

Add extra lecturer seats and student blocks, or ask us for a custom quote for the faculty or the whole university.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices exclude VAT, and VAT invoices are available.
