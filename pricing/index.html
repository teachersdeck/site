Pricing

Simple pricing for every classroom

Choose who it's for, then the plan. Prices are in VND, excluding VAT. Yearly plans are 10 months, so two months are free.

Request a quoteBook a demo

Plans

Teachers

  • Free (Try it in class): 0 VND, free forever. The everyday classroom widgets: clock, timers, traffic light, dice, checklist, calendar and more; Runs in your browser or as your Chrome new tab; English and Tiếng Việt; Your classes stay on your own device. No account needed.
  • TeachersDeck Pro (For one teacher): 240,000 VND a month or 2,100,000 VND a year, per teacher. Every widget: DrawDeck whiteboard, Lesson Planner, Games, Poll, Exit Ticket and more; Cloud Class: students and parents join with a code; Notes home and progress reports for parents; Founding Teacher: the first 100 teachers pay 1,300,000 VND a year. Already included for every teacher on a school or center plan.
  • ClassIn or Canvas (Inside your teaching platform): 1,250,000 VND a year, per teacher. TeachersDeck on your ClassIn board, opened with one link; Or launched from any Canvas course, signed in through Canvas; Widgets that show no student data on a shared screen; Free live demo, no account needed. Centers and schools with 10 or more teachers get a written quote.

Schools & centers

  • Suite Starter (A small center or one department): 1,500,000 VND a month or 15,000,000 VND a year, per campus. Up to 8 teachers and 150 students; TeachersDeck Pro for every teacher; StudentDeck, ParentDeck and AdminDeck; 2 admin accounts.
  • Suite Standard (Most campuses): 3,000,000 VND a month or 30,000,000 VND a year, per campus. Up to 20 teachers and 400 students; TeachersDeck Pro for every teacher; StudentDeck, ParentDeck and AdminDeck; 5 admin accounts. Extra teacher 90,000 VND a month; extra 50 students 150,000 VND a month.
  • Suite Growth (Larger schools): 5,200,000 VND a month or 52,000,000 VND a year, per campus. Up to 40 teachers and 900 students; TeachersDeck Pro for every teacher; StudentDeck, ParentDeck and AdminDeck; Unlimited admin accounts. Bigger than this? We send a custom quote.

Try it free for 30 days. Join through the pilot and your plan is 30% off for the first 12 months.

Groups of 4 or more campuses or faculties get 10% to 20% off, and 5% more on a two-year agreement.

International schools and universities also pay yearly seats: 100,000 VND per student and 500,000 VND per parent who links. Private schools and language centers pay no seat fees.

Only need the teacher app? TeachersDeck campus plans start at 800,000 VND a month.

Families

  • ParentDeck Free (One child): 0 VND, free forever. Follow one child's points, homework and notes from the teacher; Read-only: nothing to set up or manage; Your child's own private code.
  • ParentDeck Pro (For each child): 30,000 VND a month or 300,000 VND a year, per child. Badge Display Case, Homework insights and Rewards Wishlist; Conversation Starters, Weekly Digest and Class Pulse; Also unlocks your child's StudentDeck Pro; Brothers and sisters: 2nd child 30% off, 3rd and more 50% off. Founding Family: the first 200 children get 30% off their first year.

If your child's school plan already pays for StudentDeck, you don't pay for it again.
