Questions from language centers

Do language centers pay seat fees?

No. Language centers pay the campus plan only, whether they run one center or fifty. Seat fees apply only to international schools.

When do volume discounts start?

At 4 campuses: 10% off from 4 to 9, 15% off with premium support from 10 to 24, and 20% off or better from 25. A two-year agreement takes another 5% off.

We have 50 or more centers. What do we pay?

Chains of 25 or more get a custom quote at 20% off list or better. Tell us your number of centers, teachers and students and we'll prepare it.

Can we try it before we commit?

Yes. The 30-day free pilot gives up to 5 classrooms, free setup and no card needed. Centers that join through the pilot get 30% off the campus plan for 12 months.

Can each center have a different plan?

Yes. Each campus picks the plan that fits its teachers and students; the volume discount applies to the list price of each one.
