What it is

A version of TeachersDeck that Canvas launches like any other course tool.

Opens from the course

TeachersDeck appears in the Canvas course menu. One click opens the deck, ready to show on the classroom screen.

Signed in through Canvas

Canvas tells TeachersDeck who the teacher is, using the LTI 1.3 standard, so there is no separate login or password.

Teachers only

Teachers and teaching assistants get the deck. Students who open it see a short note to ask their teacher.

No roster or gradebook access

TeachersDeck asks Canvas for nothing beyond the launch: it never reads your class lists, grades or submissions.

TeachersDeck for Canvas questions

Who has to set it up?

Your Canvas admin, once, for the whole institution. After that every teacher finds TeachersDeck in their courses.

What does TeachersDeck see from Canvas?

Only what a launch needs: who the teacher is, their role and their language. It requests no access to rosters, grades or submissions.

Do students use it?

Not in this edition. Students see the deck on the classroom screen; StudentDeck and ParentDeck connected to Canvas are planned after the pilot.

Is it the same as the TeachersDeck Chrome extension?

It is the same widgets, launched from Canvas instead of the browser's new tab. Already have TeachersDeck Pro? Ask us about adding Canvas.
