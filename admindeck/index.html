AdminDeck questions

How do we get AdminDeck?

AdminDeck is included in every TeachersDeck Suite campus plan, from 1,500,000 VND a month.

Can leaders see individual students?

School Stats shows totals and trends without student names. Directories list people so leaders can manage rosters.

Does it work for chains?

Yes. Groups of 4 or more campuses get one contract, one invoice and a group overview, with 10% to 20% off list price.

Which languages does it support?

AdminDeck works in English and Vietnamese.
