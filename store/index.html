How buying works

A Pro plan from the deck, or themes, faces and docks from the grid.

Scan the code in any Vietnamese banking app. Keep the order code in the transfer note.

Questions

How do I pay?

By VietQR bank transfer from any Vietnamese bank. The order code (like TD K7Q4M9) must stay in the transfer note so we can match your payment. Buying from abroad? Request a quote and we send an invoice.

When do I get my key?

As soon as we confirm the transfer: we check the account every day. Keep this page open, or come back later: your order is remembered in this browser, and the key is emailed too.

How does the key reach the app?

The page and the email give you a link such as teacher.myteachersdeck.com/#redeem=KEY. Open it and Pro (or your Shop items) switches on. You can also type the key in the app: TeachersDeck → Settings → Pro, or Theme → Shop → I have a store key.

What is a store key?

Themes, faces and docks bought here go on a store key (SK-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Enter it on any computer and your items come with it. Buy more later and they are added to the same key.

Can my child buy something?

No. StudentDeck never shows a price. Children get Pro through their family's ParentDeck Pro or their school's plan.

Buying for a whole school?

One plan covers every teacher and family, with AdminDeck Pro for the office. We reply by email and Zalo within one working day.
