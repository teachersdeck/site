Questions from international schools

What are seat fees?

International schools pay 100,000 VND per student and 500,000 VND per parent each year, on top of the campus plan. Private schools and language centers do not pay seat fees.

Who pays for student licences?

The school, through the Suite campus plan. Families never pay twice for the same child; ParentDeck Pro stays optional.

Can we try before we pay?

Yes. The 30-day free pilot gives up to 5 classrooms, free setup and no card needed.

What does the founding rate mean?

Schools that join through the pilot get 30% off the campus plan for 12 months. Suite Starter at the founding rate is 1,050,000 VND a month.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices exclude VAT, and VAT invoices are available.
