ParentDeck questions

How do I link to my child?

Sign in with Google and enter your child's family code, which the teacher or your child's StudentDeck gives you.

How much does ParentDeck cost?

ParentDeck Free covers one child. Pro is 30,000 VND per child per month or 300,000 VND per child per year, with discounts for brothers and sisters.

Can I change anything in the class?

No. ParentDeck is read-only, so it never changes your child's work, points or the teacher's records.

Does it work in Vietnamese?

Yes. ParentDeck works in English and Vietnamese, and its calendar shows lunar dates.
