TeachersDeck questions

Is TeachersDeck free?

Yes. The free plan covers the everyday classroom tools. Pro costs 240,000 VND a month or 2,100,000 VND a year, and the first 100 teachers can lock in 1,300,000 VND a year.

What is a classroom deck?

One screen of ready-to-use teaching tools. TeachersDeck puts more than 40 classroom widgets, from timers and a name picker to reward points and a whiteboard, on a single wall that opens as your Chrome new tab.

What do I need to run it?

Chrome on Windows, Mac or a Chromebook. TeachersDeck opens as your new tab page.

Do my students need accounts?

No. If you use Cloud Class, students join with a class code and a PIN, with no email address.

Does my school pay instead?

On a school or center campus plan, TeachersDeck Pro is included for every teacher seat.
